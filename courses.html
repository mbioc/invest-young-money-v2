Before you begin

A few helpful answers.

Still deciding? Send us your question.

Where do I enroll?

Use the Outschool or Udemy links above. You can review the current course information and complete enrollment on the learning platform.

Which ages are the classes for?

Age guidance varies by class. Review the individual Outschool listing for learner ages and requirements. For Money Moves, review the intended audience and requirements on Udemy.

How much do courses cost?

Visit the relevant platform for current pricing and enrollment options. Prices and availability may change, so check the listing before you enroll.

Are the classes live or self-paced?

Check the format on the specific listing before enrolling. It will explain how the course is delivered and whether you need to attend at scheduled times.

Can we arrange learning for our organization?

Yes. Book an intake call to discuss your learners, goals, timing, and group size. We can explore workshops, programs, or curriculum options with you.

Do I need an appointment to buy resources?

No. You can browse learning resources in the shop or explore course enrollment directly through the linked platforms.
