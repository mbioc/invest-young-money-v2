The Financial Foundations Framework.

Five modules that connect financial concepts to decisions employees face in everyday life.

01

Budgeting & Cash Flow Systems

Understand income, expenses, and ways to build a workable spending plan.

02

Credit, Debt & Financial Decisions

Explore credit use, borrowing, and tradeoffs behind common financial choices.

03

Savings & Emergency Funds

Set realistic savings goals and prepare for unexpected expenses.

04

Banking, Insurance & Protection

Review core banking and protection concepts that support informed decisions.

05

Investing Basics & Retirement

Learn foundational investing and retirement concepts for long-term planning.

Bonus topic

Student Loans & College Cost Planning

Discuss education costs, borrowing, and planning questions for families.

Ways to learn

Training that fits your team.

Choose live virtual or in-person learning. Formats include lunch-and-learns, interactive workshops, and multi-session programs. Speaking and keynote sessions can bring the same practical perspective to a larger audience.

For federal employees

Federal employee retirement education can introduce plan terminology and questions to consider. Any session scope is agreed in advance; individual benefit, investment, tax, and legal decisions remain with each employee and their qualified advisers.

For schools and community groups

We also support youth workshops, after-school and summer programs, and curriculum licensing conversations. Ask about curriculum licensing.
