What are your Life Plans?

Take charge of your own life, no matter who you are or where you come from, you have the ability to LEAD YOUR LIFE based on the decisions you make today.

Invest Young MoneySeptember 15, 20241 min read

Take charge of your own life, no matter who you are or where you come from, you have the ability to LEAD YOUR LIFE based on the decisions you make today.  All it takes is a plan and one step at a time. Remember some progress is better than no progress!  

If you can imagine living a life on your terms instead of by chance what would that look like for you.  Let's make a plan to sit down right now and at the beginning of every new year and paint this picture of what you need to be doing to take control!  What do you need to move you in the direction of living your life on your terms.  Let's set a few goals for the following areas and set a reminder on your calendar to review these quarterly or bi-annually.  So here it is:

-Work

-Family

-Health and wellness

-Spiritually

-Financially 

-Hobbies

Some examples might be scheduling one night out a month with family, or working on continuing your education in a field that you would like to work in or move-up in.  For health and wellness this could be working out three times a week, or working on reducing debt to eliminate financial stress.  Whatever it is that you dream, let's make it a plan and do it!!
