We’re Invest Young Money. We help young people, families, and communities make sense of money—and see what’s possible with it.

Bring learning to your community

A life skill.
A world of opportunity.

Money decisions show up long before adulthood. A first savings goal, a purchase to think through, or a business idea can become a meaningful place to start learning.

Through workshops, curriculum, and coaching, we make financial literacy and entrepreneurship approachable. Our mission is to help learners turn understanding into practical habits, informed choices, and greater confidence.

Our vision is a future where financial knowledge and opportunity are within reach for every generation.

Meet Natelya.

Educator. Founder. Community advocate.

Natelya Hale founded Invest Young Money to make financial education practical for youth, families, and working adults.

She brings 25 years of federal public service and leadership to her work. Her qualifications include Certified Financial Coach, Master of Public Administration, a Georgia Life and Health Insurance license, and Youth and Adult Mental Health certifications.

Natelya has teaching experience in financial literacy, entrepreneurship, and life skills. She brings that experience to learners in schools, community programs, and workplaces.
