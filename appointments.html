Which appointment should I choose?

If your school or organization is exploring a program, start with the free intake call. Choose the strategy session for detailed program planning, or the Financial Education Consultation for individual and family learning goals.

Does the free call include program design?

The intake call is a short discovery conversation. Program design, detailed planning, and curriculum review are outside its scope.

Can I get resources without an appointment?

Yes. Our printable resources and curriculum bundles can be explored directly. Browse learning resources to find a starting point.
