Who are your programs for?

Programs are designed for corporations, banks and credit unions, colleges and universities, churches, schools, community organizations, families, and individual learners. We serve children, teens, and adults; learning resources are organized by elementary, middle school, and high school stages.

How do we book a workshop?

Start with a free 15-minute Program Intake Call to discuss your audience and what you want learners to take away. Visit our appointment page to choose the right session.

Book an Appointment
Can we learn at home?

Yes. Explore our printable financial literacy resources and bundles, or discover classes taught by Natelya Hale on Outschool.

Explore Outschool classes
Can our organization license the curriculum?

Curriculum licensing is available for schools and organizations. Request licensing information to discuss the right option for your program.
